Daily Habits
🥩 Protein first at every meal. 83–104g/day. Aim 20–30g per meal. Greek yogurt, eggs, fish, chicken.
💪 Resistance training 3×/week. GLP-1s take ~25% from lean mass without this. Bodyweight counts.
🚶 8,000 steps/day. Endocrinologist-recommended floor for GLP-1 users.
💧 2.5–3L water daily. Helps constipation and nausea significantly.
🍽️ Stop at 70% full. Eat slowly — override satiety and nausea follows fast.
🚫 No fatty/spicy food on injection day +24hrs. Biggest nausea trigger.
😴 7–8hrs sleep. Poor sleep raises ghrelin and counters appetite suppression.
🍷 Cut alcohol. Blocks fat oxidation 24–48hrs and overrides fullness cues.

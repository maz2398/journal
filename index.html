Daily Habits
🥩 Protein first at every meal. 83–104g/day. Aim 20–30g per meal before anything else. Non-fat Greek yogurt, eggs, fish, chicken.
💪 Resistance training 3×/week. GLP-1s can take ~25% of weight from lean mass without this. Bodyweight counts.
🚶 8,000 steps/day. The endocrinologist-recommended floor for GLP-1 users. Not a workout — just movement.
💧 2.5–3L water daily. Helps constipation and nausea significantly. Add electrolytes if you feel weak.
🍽️ Stop at 70% full. Eat slowly. Wegovy amplifies satiety — override it and nausea follows fast.
🚫 No fatty/spicy food on injection day +24hrs. Biggest nausea trigger. Light, protein-rich meals those days.
😴 7–8hrs sleep. Poor sleep raises ghrelin (hunger hormone) and directly counters the drug's appetite suppression.
🍷 Cut alcohol for now. Blocks fat oxidation for 24–48hrs and overrides fullness cues. Mocktails for social occasions.
